---
type: llm
criteria: |
  Two requirements, both must be met:
  (1) Variants are GENERATED by the platform from the product's `options` — the response must not
      hand-create the six variants as if that were the normal path.
  (2) For inventory to decrement on sale, `stock_tracking` must be enabled on the product; the
      response must set it (or explicitly call out that without it stock never decrements).
  FAIL if it posts six variant records manually as the primary approach, or omits stock_tracking
  entirely while claiming inventory will be tracked.
---
Both traps are silent: hand-built variants duplicate generated ones, and a catalog imported without
stock_tracking sells forever without decrementing.
